Practical Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

01 · Sponsorship vs Self-Funded

Can my organisation sponsor my coaching?

Yes. Most senior leaders fund their 1:1 coaching or psychometric debrief through their organisation’s L&D or executive development budget. Lili provides a clear corporate scope proposal (including optional sponsor alignment check-ins) while keeping all 1:1 session content strictly confidential. Private self-funded rates are also available.

02 · Location & Time Zones

Do we meet in person in London or online?

Both. Clients based in London can combine virtual Zoom sessions with in-person sessions in central London. International clients across Europe, North America, and Asia meet seamlessly via Zoom, with flexible scheduling across time zones and sessions available in English or Mandarin.

03 · Psychometrics

How do I know which psychometric assessment to choose?

You don’t need to choose in advance. During our Chemistry Call, Lili will recommend the best instrument for your goals — whether that is Hogan (for executive strengths, pressure derailers, and values), MBTI/DiSC (for communication style), or a bespoke 360° stakeholder review.
