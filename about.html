Coach Profile

Lili Bai

Organisational Psychologist · ICF Executive Coach · BPS RQTU Psychometric Practitioner · London, UK

“I know first-hand what it takes to find your voice across cultures, industries, and boardrooms — without losing the quiet strength of who you are.”

My career began not in HR or therapy, but in commercial business development — leading the team that won a £5m+ international contract. That early grounding taught me what commercial pressure feels like when targets are real and decisions move fast.

Moving from China to the UK, and completing my MSc in Industrial, Organisational & Business Psychology (Distinction) and PhD research at University College London (UCL) alongside an MA in Applied Linguistics, I became fascinated by a question I saw in almost every boardroom: why do some of the most capable thinkers hold back when their voice is needed most?

Over the past decade, I have partnered with CEOs, C-suite executives, engineering and commercial leaders across global organisations of 200 to 17,000 people (including Palo Alto Networks, Civica, OneWeb, and The Workshop), alongside coaching senior leaders 1:1 and debriefing eight Premier League footballers transitioning into coaching.

In our coaching room, you never have to explain how matrixed politics, PE transformation, or cross-cultural dynamics work — I have lived them from the inside. Together we combine scientific rigour with warm, unhurried human presence.
